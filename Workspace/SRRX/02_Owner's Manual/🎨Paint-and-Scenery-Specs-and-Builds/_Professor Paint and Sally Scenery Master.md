# SRRX SCENERY, LANDSCAPE, & 3D PRINTING STANDARDS
# Authorities: Professor Paint & Sally Scenery

## 1. ENVIRONMENTAL GEOGRAPHY
* **Setting:** Rocky Mountain region (High alpine passes, rugged cliffs, and deep gorges).
* **Primary Scenic Features:** Pine Valley District, Timberline District, Silver Ridge District, and the West Wall Gorge run.
* **The Mine Spur Profile:** Located on the North Leg loop. The track climbs sharply on custom risers/grades to reach high-altitude mineral scenery. 
    * *Critical Clearance Window:* At its closest crossing point over the surface-linkage system of Turnout #3, the bottom of the elevated Mine Spur track structure maintains exactly 0.7" of vertical clearance above the top surface of the 1" pink foam base.

## 2. 3D DESIGN & FABRICATION PARAMETERS
* **Material Standard:** PLA (Polylactic Acid) filament used for all structural custom components.
* **Core Modeling Targets:** Prototypical Sears-style houses, lineside track shanties, custom figures, and mountain industrial structures.
* **Surface Preparation Protocol:** Raw PLA prints must be sanded, treated with fill-primer to eliminate print layer lines, and sealed before applying final color coats.

## 3. FINISHING & PAINTING CODES
* **Equipment:** Airbrush, aerosol spray coatings, and precision hand-brushing.
* **Track Appearance:** Weathered grimy black/rust rail profiles. Ground linkages and surface torsion wires must be painted flat grimy black or disguised beneath lineside infrastructure (ballast, tool sheds) to match the surrounding landscape.
