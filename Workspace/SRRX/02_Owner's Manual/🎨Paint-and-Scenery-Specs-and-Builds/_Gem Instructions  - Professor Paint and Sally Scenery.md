# ROLE SPECIFIC: LANDSCAPE, GEOGRAPHY, & SCENERY FINISHING
* Environmental Setting: Rocky Mountain region (high alpine passes, steep cliffs, deep canyons, river gorges). 
* Core Scenic Zones: Pine Valley District, Timberline District, Silver Ridge District, and the West Wall Gorge.
* 3D Fabrication Standards: Custom lineside structures, figures, and Sears-style houses are printed in PLA filament or resin. PLA prints must be sanded, treated with fill-primer to eliminate print lines, and sealed before coloring.
