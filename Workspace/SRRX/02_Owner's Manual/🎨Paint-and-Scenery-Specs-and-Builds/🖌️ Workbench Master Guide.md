## 1. Batch Priming Strategy & Equipment Setup
### Grouping Models by Primer Type & Mounting
- Group 1: FDM 3D Prints (High-Build / Fill Primer)
	- Items: Curved bridges, yard house, spare 3D house, fuel depot propane tanks.
	- Technique: Spray warm passes of sandable fill-primer (e.g., Rust-Oleum Auto Fill-Primer) to level layer lines. Wet-sand with 600–800 grit before applying color.
- Group 2: Fine Resin & Detail Parts (Ultra-Thin Micro Primer)
	- Items: Wildlife (elk, moose, bears), gooseneck lamps, picnic tables, gas station accessories (pumps, tires, oil drums), satellite dish.
	- Technique: Mount tiny pieces on wooden stirrers/popsicle sticks using blue tack or double-sided tape. Mist fine surface primer (Tamiya Fine Surface Primer or Vallejo Surface Primer) at 15–18 PSI to avoid clogging fine fur/surface textures.
- Group 3: Vehicles & Figures (Tack-Mounted Batching)
	- Items: Suburbans, Jeep, vintage pickup, Stay Tuned barbershop quartet figures (O scale & N scale).
	- Technique: Elevate vehicle bodies on foam blocks to paint under rocker panels and bumpers. Apply white/light grey primer on figures; keep passes light to preserve facial details.
## 2. Airbrushing & Paint Booth Parameters
### Compressor & Nozzle Configurations
- Settings Calibration:
	- Setting 1 (Low ~10–15 PSI): Precision detail, fine line shading, thin washes, close-up weathering.
	- Setting 2 (Medium ~18–22 PSI): Primary workhorse for smooth coverage, structure base coats.
	- Setting 3 (High ~25–30 PSI): High-build fill-primers, thick varnishes, broad terrain bases.
- Nozzle Selection:
	- $0.2\text{ mm}$ Nozzle: Fine detail, exhaust trails, tiny detail parts, scale rust streaks. Requires well-thinned paint - (milk consistency).
	- $0.3\text{ mm}$ Nozzle: General N-scale work (structures, vehicles, broad surfaces).
	- $0.5\text{ mm}$ Nozzle: Fill-primers, matte clear topcoats, large FDM prints.
### Paint Systems & Thinning
- Micro-Mark Acrylics & Railroad Colors: Add 1–2 drops of acrylic thinner/flow improver per 10 drops of paint in the airbrush cup to prevent tip-dry.
- Vallejo Greens: Use Gunship Green for muted, earthy structural bodies/foliage and Signal Green for pop accents (awnings, gas station trim).
- Vallejo Liquid Mask: Apply over fully cured base coats (12–24 hrs) using an old brush or toothpick. Allow 15–30 minutes to turn translucent before airbrushing topcoat. Peel within 1–2 hours.
## 3. Weathering, Washes & Finishing Techniques
### India Ink & Alcohol (IIA) Wash
- Formula: Black India ink thinned into Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA).
- Application:
	- *Mortar & Shadows:* Brush lightly over brick textures or structure walls; capillary action draws ink into recessed mortar and shadow lines.
	- *Wood & Concrete:* Apply over light grey/tan bases to convert fresh timber to sun-bleached wood (picnic tables, decks). Wipe across grey concrete pads for oil drips and expansion joints.
	- *Track & Bridges:* Wash over ties and bridge abutments to simulate creosote and heavy soot.
- Control: Touch brush to a paper towel first to prevent flooding. Reactivate/clean up excess with a clean brush dipped in plain IPA.
### Micro-Mark Weathering Powders
- **Surface Prep:** Always work over a cured matte finish or flat paint base to give pigments physical bite.
- **Dry Brushing:** Tap off 90% of powder on a towel; scrub gently into lower walls and wheel wells for kicked-up dirt.
- **Vertical Dragging:** Drag dark gray/soot powders downward from window corners, roof overhangs, and chimneys using a flat brush.
- **Powder Wash:** Mix powder with 70% IPA for pin-washes in deep crevices.
- **Sealing:** Lock powders with a light mist pass of airbrush matte finish ($0.5\text{ mm}$ nozzle at Medium PSI, 10–12 inches away).

### Commercial & Government Buildings
- **Elk River Outfitters:** Unpainted textured brick side panels enhanced with light mortar wash; rear PLA wall dull-coated with vertical soot streaks.
- **Elk River Community Bank:** Two-tone gray stonework lower facade with cream upper story; anchored at north block corner.
Federal Building Compound:
- **Satellite Dish:** Seal paper/wood umbrella with matte clear/CA glue, spray Dark Dull Gray / Parchment, metallic feed horn, dark grey rib wash.
- **Chain-Link & Concertina Wire:** Dark Gray/Galvanized fence mesh with black wash; steel wire coils primed grey, touched with rust wash at post bases.
- **Government Suburbans:** Dark Charcoal / Scale Black, gloss black window glass, silver headlights, light tan dust wash on rocker panels.
### Tourist Stop & Gorge Zone
- **Flagstop Depot:** Paint underside of deep porch roof off-white/cream to bounce light from installed warm-white LED. Dull silver corrugated metal roofs with dark grey wash and rust seams.
- **Gorge-ous Grub (Caboose Diner):** Black pin-wash in panel seams; dark soot powder around roof exhaust stack.
Scratch-Built Accessories:
- **Trash Cans:** Cut short sections of $3/32''$ or $1/8''$ round styrene rod, paint dark green/galvanized, stipple black top for trash bag liner.
- **Patio Umbrellas:** Punch $1/2''$ paper circles, slit to center, overlap to form shallow cone; mount on brass wire/toothpick posts; paint in matte canvas tones.
