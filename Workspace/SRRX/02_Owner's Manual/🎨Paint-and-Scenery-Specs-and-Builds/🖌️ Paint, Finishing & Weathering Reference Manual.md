# 1. Equipment Setup & Airbrush Operating Parameters

## Compressor Pressure Guide
- **Low (~10–15 PSI):** Fine-line shading, scale rust streaks, exhaust trails, close-up pin-point weathering, and thin washes.
- **Medium (~18–22 PSI):** Standard operating setting. Base-coating structures, rolling stock, vehicles, and general acrylic spraying.
- **High (~25–30 PSI):** High-build fill-primers, thick acrylic varnishes/matte clear coats, and broad terrain/base-deck coverage.
## Nozzle Selection
- **$0.2\text{ mm}$ Nozzle (Fine Detail):** Micro-shading, fine weathering streaks, vehicle panels, and figures. Requires well-thinned paint (skim-milk consistency) to prevent tip-dry.
- **$0.3\text{ mm}$ Nozzle (General Benchwork):** Primary workhorse for N scale structures, Sears houses, locomotives, and base coats. Offers optimal balance between coverage and spray control.
- **$0.5\text{ mm}$ Nozzle (Heavy Coverage):** High-build primers, flat clear varnishes, large 3D prints, and scenery bases. Minimizes tip-dry with heavier-bodied mediums.
## Thinning & Additive Ratios
- **Airbrush Thinning (Micro-Mark Airbrush & Basic Sets):** Mix roughly **3:1 to 4:1** (paint to 29312 / 29294 Thinner). Add **1–2 drops of 29313 Flow Improver** per 10 drops of paint to eliminate tip-dry.
- **Brush Painting:** Start at a **10:1** ratio (10 drops paint to 1 drop 29294 Thinner) to eliminate brush strokes on lap siding and flat panels without reducing pigment opacity.
- **Vallejo Liquid Mask:** Apply with an old brush or toothpick over a cured base coat (12–24 hours). Allow 15–30 minutes to dry translucent before spraying secondary trim. Peel within 1–2 hours using tweezers or poster tack.
- **Tool Cleaning (29295 Cleaner):** Flush the airbrush cup immediately between colors. Swirl detail brushes in cleaner and rinse with clean water to prevent pigment buildup in the ferrule.

### Blending & Batching Tips
- **Repeatable Ratios:** Use disposable plastic pipettes or eye-droppers to count drops for small touch-up jars, or mark volume gradients on mixing cups for larger airbrush batches.
- **Thinning Post-Mix:** Always blend and stir your pigments thoroughly **before** adding thinner or flow improver so the pigment carrier stays balanced.
# 3. Weathering Powders, Inks & Chemical Washes
## Micro-Mark 12-Color Weathering Powder Guide
- **Rusty Brown:** Settled corrosion on trucks, coupler pockets, rail webs, and lower edges of corrugated siding.
- **Rusty Red:** Active surface rust on roof seams, exhaust stacks, brake gear, and metal water runoff lines.
- **Grimy Black:** Heavy carbon deposits on diesel exhausts, diner vent flues, chimney tops, and tunnel portals.
- **Grungy Gray:** Faded industrial grime across asphalt roads, concrete platforms, and bridge abutments.
- **Dirty Brown:** Wet soil and road splash scrubbed into wheel wells, lower chassis, and high-traffic wooden steps.
- **Muddy Red:** Mineral-rich red clay for gorge cut-banks, drainage ditches, and river erosion.
- **Desert Sand:** Arid dust for lower freight car sills, vehicle rocker panels, and gravel parking lots.
- **Highlight White:** Hard-water stains, chalking below window sills/downspouts, and concrete weep-hole leaching.
- **Mildew Green:** Damp organic growth on shaded foundations, gorge rock walls, and wooden trestle pilings.
- **Patina Green:** Verdigris copper/bronze oxidation on bank trim, roof valleys, and exterior lighting fixtures.
- **Faded Blue:** Sun-bleached paint fading across blue rolling stock roofs, vehicle hoods, and residential siding.
- **Gritty Yellow:** Faded signage, yellow maintenance-of-way equipment, and crossing gates.
## Powder Application & Fixative Protocol
- **Surface Prep:** Apply only over a fully cured **matte finish** or flat paint; glossy surfaces will not retain dry pigment.
- **Liquid Wash Technique:** Mix a pinch of powder into **70% Isopropyl Alcohol** for a fast-drying capillary pin-wash.
- **Sealing:** Mist a light pass of airbrush matte finish ($0.5\text{ mm}$ nozzle, medium pressure, 10–12 inches away). Apply dry powders roughly 15% lighter than desired, as matte clear coats deepen pigment tone.
## India Ink & Alcohol (IIA) Wash
- **Formula:** Higgins/Speedball Black India Ink thinned into 70–91% Isopropyl Alcohol.
- **Mortar Lines & Recesses:** Flood lightly over brick and stone textures; capillary action pulls pigment directly into mortar joints.
- **Wood Aging:** Apply over light gray or buff bases on decks, trestles, and depot platforms to simulate sun-bleached, aged lumber.
- **Concrete & Asphalt:** Wipe lightly across gray pads to create oil stains, tire marks, and expansion cracks.
- **Application Control:** Touch brush tip to a paper towel before applying to prevent pooling. Clean up or dilute over-concentrated areas immediately with a clean brush dipped in pure IPA.

- **Federal Building Compound:**
    - _Satellite Dish:_
	    - **Primer:** * Apply a light mist of neutral **Gray Primer** (airbrush or fine spray).
	        - This provides the necessary mechanical bite on the smooth 3D print and covers any faint layer steps in the reflector bowl.
        - **Dish Bowl (Main Face & Back):**
	        - Spray a flat **Light Gray / Reefer Gray** or an off-white (such as _Aged Concrete_ or _Reefer White_ toned down with a drop of gray).
            - _Why avoid pure white?_ Pure bright white reflects too harshly in N scale and draws unwanted visual weight away from the rest of the structure.
        - **Feed Horn & Support Struts (The Spider Legs):**
	        - Use a 000 detail brush or a sharp toothpick to pick out the central feed horn and thin support rods in **Sooty Black** or **Tarnished Black** (or dark graphite/gunmetal).
            - Having the struts dark provides crisp visual contrast against the lighter reflector bowl, making those delicate resin lines pop at 1:160 scale.
        - **Mast & Roof Base Flange:**
	        - Paint the vertical mounting pipe and bottom base plate in **Dark Industrial Gray**, **Galvanized Gray**, or **Tarnished Black**.
	        - This anchors the dish visually to the gravel or slate roof deck of the Federal Building.
		- **Weathering & Final Accents**:
		- **Subtle Grime Wash:** Run an extremely diluted dark gray wash along the inner bottom rim of the bowl where rain and grime pool naturally in real dishes.
	    - **Mast Sealing:** Once glued to the roof deck, a microscopic dab of **Grimy Black** or dark gray at the roof contact points will simulate tar flashing or a weatherproof mounting pad.
    - _Security Fence & Concertina Wire:_ Silver/galvanized mesh toned down with dark gray/black wash; 24-gauge steel wire coils primed gray with rust wash accents at post mounts.
    - _Government Suburbans:_ Dark Charcoal / Scale Black body, gloss black window glaze, silver headlights, clear red taillights, and 29025 Dirt powder on rocker panels.
