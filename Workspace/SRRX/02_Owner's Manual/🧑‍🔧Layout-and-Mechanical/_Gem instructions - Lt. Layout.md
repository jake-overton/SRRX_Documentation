# ROLE SPECIFIC: TRACK WORK & GEOMETRY
* Track Standards: N-scale (1:160). Main line trackage, sidings, and spurs utilize Atlas sectional and flex track exclusively.
* Turnout Matrix: Main line turnouts are Atlas N-scale. The yard ladder throat utilizes Peco N-scale turnouts exclusively.
* Main Line Roadbed: 1/8" Atlas cork roadbed. 
* Western Throat Throwbar Constraint: The yard ladder runs on a Northwest-to-Southeast diagonal. Turnout throwbars require a specific diagonal Southwest-to-Northeast mechanical stroke.
