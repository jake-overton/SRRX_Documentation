## Background — Why This Changed

The smelter was previously located on the east leg, requiring ore to be hauled from the mine on the north leg, through the yard, and across to the east leg — a long and operationally implausible transit that crossed the mountain. In V10, the smelter is relocated to the north leg, before the mainline crosses under the mine branch. Ore now flows directly from mine to smelter, having to pass though the yard, but eliminating the cross-mountain movement. This also creates a richer operating sequence involving the helper engine at the smelter delivery stage.

## Section 3.4 — The Silver Smelter

**What's there now:** A description of the smelter as an east leg industry, with ore arriving by transit from the mine.

**Replace entirely with:**

> The Silver Ridge Smelter sits on the lower slope of Silver Ridge Mountain, serviced by the mainline along the south side of Pine Valley Junction. The smelter predates the surrounding Elk River National Park District by decades; its operating rights are grandfathered, and the slag pile that accumulates on the south face of its site remains a point of low-grade negotiation between the railroad and park administration. The smelter does not advertise. It does not need to.
>
> Ore moves from the mine by gravity — loaded cars descend the switchbacks under control, past the smelter turnout, where the helper engine waiting there joins the consist for the final push up the smelter spur. The grade from the turnout to the smelter is steep enough that neither the road engine nor the helper attempts it alone with a full load. Once spotted at the smelter, cars are unloaded and processed. Empties return downgrade to the turnout, where the helper cuts away and returns to the helper pocket at PVJ. Processed metal leaves by the mainline in dedicated cars on a separate movement.

## Section 7.5 — Helper Engine Operating Priority — Silver Ridge Mine

**What's there now:** Helper assists with mine ascent, cuts away at the mine, returns light to helper pocket.

**Revise to reflect the extended helper role:**

> Helper engines assigned to the mine run perform two functions in a single cycle. On the uphill movement, the helper pushes the consist from the helper pocket at PVJ up the mine branch switchbacks to the Silver Ridge Mine. The helper cuts away at the mine while empties are spotted and loaded cars are picked up.
>
> On the downhill movement with the loaded consist, the helper is not required — the grade works in the train's favor and the road engine provides braking control through the switchbacks. At the base of the switchbacks, at the smelter branch turnout, the helper rejoins the consist on the uphill end. Both engines push the loaded cars up the smelter spur grade to the smelter.
>
> ***This following paragraph needs to be rewritten***
>
> The helper does not return to the yard independently while the load is being processed. It holds at the smelter until empties are released, then returns light to the helper pocket — either coupled to the road engine or running separately, at the crew's discretion. At the smelter branch turnout, the helper couples to the uphill end of the consist. Both engines push the full load up the smelter spur to the spotting position at the smelter.

## Section 8.3 — The Mine Run

**What's there now:** A movement description that likely routes ore from mine to yard to smelter in separate movements.

**Replace the mine run movement description with:**

> The mine run originates at PVJ yard with empty ore cars. The road engine and helper depart together, the helper on the uphill end, and climb the mine branch switchbacks to the Silver Ridge Mine. At the mine, the helper holds while the road engine spots empties on the loading spur and picks up loaded cars.
>
> The descent to the yard requires no helper — the loaded consist moves downgrade under the road engine's control through the switchbacks. 
>
> The road engine holds at the smelter while unloading proceeds. When empties are released, the road engine pulls the empty cars back down the smelter spur to the turnout. The helper cuts away and returns independently to the PVJ helper pocket. The road engine returns the empties to the yard to complete the cycle.
>
> Total cycle time varies with load volume and smelter throughput. In practice, the helper's hold at the smelter is the variable that most affects overall mine run duration.
