## Appendix B – Operating Timetable
The Silver Ridge Railway operates on a condensed timetable designed for single-operator sessions. The [[SRRX Handbook - Section 6|Valley Flyer]] holds priority over all freight movements on both subdivisions. Freight windows are scheduled around Flyer departures and arrivals to prevent conflicts at Pine Valley Junction, the NE tunnels, and Silver Ridge. All times and train numbers below are provisional pending timetable formalization.

### B.1. Valley Flyer — Gorge Subdivision
The Valley Flyer operates over the Gorge Subdivision (counterclockwise) in both directions. Departure times reflect fast-clock session time; the conversion ratio is to be established at commissioning.

* **Westbound — Pine Valley Junction to Silver Ridge**: Train SRRX- departs Pine Valley Junction at 00:00, proceeding via the station spur, inner NE tunnel, National Park District, Timberline Fuel Depot, and Silver Ridge Gorge, arriving Silver Ridge Depot at 00:00.
* **Eastbound — Silver Ridge to Pine Valley Junction**: Train SRRX- departs Silver Ridge Depot at 00:00, returning via the exact reverse of the westbound route, arriving Pine Valley Junction at 00:00.

### B.2. Freight Operations — River Subdivision
River Run freight operates clockwise: Pine Valley Junction eastbound via the outer NE tunnel, south through Timberline Lumber, arriving Silver Ridge from the east. The River Run freight window opens after the Valley Flyer has cleared PVJ and closes before the Flyer's return.

* Train SRRX- departs Pine Valley Junction at 00:00, works Timberline Lumber, and arrives Silver Ridge at 00:00.

### B.3. Freight Operations — Mine Run
The Mine Run operates off the north leg, departing PVJ yard and working the Silver Ridge Mine switchback. The mine run is scheduled within the freight window and must clear the north leg before any mainline movement through the NE corner.

* Train SRRX- departs PVJ yard at 00:00, works mine spurs A and B, and returns to PVJ at 00:00.

### B.4. Freight Operations — Gorge Subdivision
Gorge Subdivision freight serves the Timberline Fuel Depot. This assignment operates only when the Valley Flyer is not occupying the subdivision. Scheduling must account for the Flyer's turnaround time at Silver Ridge.

* Train SRRX- departs Pine Valley Junction at 00:00, works Timberline Fuel Depot, and returns to PVJ at 00:00.

### B.5. D&RGW Interchange
Interchange cuts are set out and picked up at the NW corner interchange track. Interchange switching is conducted by MoPac #4637 within the PVJ yard limits and does not require a separate train authority. Timing is coordinated with the local freight window.

* Interchange window: 00:00 – 00:00

### B.6. Silver Ridge Town Switching
Silver Ridge yard and depot switching is performed within yard limits by the road locomotive after arrival. No separate authority is required. The team track and depot spur are worked as needed within the turnaround window.

* Silver Ridge switching window: 00:00 – 00:00

### B.7. Notes
All train numbers, departure times, and arrival times are pending formalization. Fast-clock ratio and session length are to be established at commissioning. Meet points and passing sidings are to be confirmed following track laying and fouling point installation per Section 7.9. This timetable shell is intended as a structural reference only; a completed timetable will be inserted here prior to first full operations session.
