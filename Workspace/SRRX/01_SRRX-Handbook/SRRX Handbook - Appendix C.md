## Appendix C – Waybill Reference and Car Forwarding Guide
This appendix provides the car forwarding reference for all SRRX freight operations. Specific car assignments, commodity designations, and spot numbers are to be completed following track laying, fouling point installation, and fleet decoder commissioning. The two-visit cycle governs all freight car movements; see SECTION 8 for operating procedures by assignment.

### C.1. The Two-Visit Cycle
Prose description of the two-visit cycle as applied to SRRX waybill operations — how cars are spotted on the first visit and retrieved on the second, and how waybills are rotated between sessions.

### C.2. Industry Spots and Car Assignments
* **Silver Ridge Mine**: Spur A (loaded/active) and Spur B (empties). Car types, spot numbers, and commodity designations to be assigned at commissioning.
* **Timberline Lumber**: Single spur, second spot pending construction. Car types and commodity designations to be assigned at commissioning.
* **Timberline Fuel Depot**: Primary and secondary tank car spots. Car assignments and spot numbers to be confirmed at commissioning.
* **Silver Smelter**: Tank car spots. Car assignments and spot numbers to be confirmed at commissioning.
* **Silver Ridge Depot team track**: General freight. Configuration and spot numbers pending construction.
* **D&RGW Interchange**: Inbound and outbound car designations to be established following interchange track confirmation at construction.

### C.3. Waybill Format and Rotation
Description of physical or digital waybill format used on the SRRX, and the session-to-session rotation procedure.

### C.4. Car Forwarding Reference
Master car forwarding table — one entry per car: reporting marks, car type, assigned industry, spot number, commodity, and load state. To be completed at commissioning.
