# APPENDIX D – SRRX Logo and Colors

This appendix provides the official color palette and logo variants for the Silver Ridge Railway. These standards govern locomotive paint, rolling stock lettering, signage, and all printed and digital materials carrying the SRRX identity. Logo image files are to be placed by the publisher in the designated positions below.

## D.1. Official Color Palette
* **SRRX Maroon — Hex #5A1F1F**: Applied to locomotive body panels and logo background fields.
* **SRRX Cream — Hex #F2E6C9**: Applied to lettering, striping, and herald text on locomotives and passenger equipment.
* **Safety Yellow — Hex #EED202**: Applied to handrails, grab irons, and safety markings on all motive power.
* **Deep Black — Hex #000000**: Applied to underframes, walkways, and trucks on all equipment.
* **Gray — Hex #A8A8A8**: Applied to stenciled reporting marks and data panels on freight cars.

## D.2. Logo Variants
The SRRX maintains four logo variants for use across different applications. All variants share the core mountain, treeline, and winding road illustration with SRRX lettering. Selection of the appropriate variant is governed by the surface, scale, and reproduction context.

* **Warm Gray**: The primary circular badge with maroon field, cream arc text reading Silver Ridge Railway, and Safety Yellow SRRX on a black oval center. Used for official documents, printed materials, and display signage.
* **Official Badge**: Shield and oval badge format with maroon field and cream border. Proportioned for application to locomotive cab panels and tender sides.
* **Locomotive Variant**: Similar oval shield format, adjusted proportions for freight and passenger car application.
* **Rolling Stock Variant**: High-contrast black, white, and maroon treatment for freight car reporting marks and monochrome print contexts.
* **Stencil Variant**: Distressed treatment of the Official Badge for use on layout signage, fascia, and scenic detail applications where an aged appearance is appropriate.

## D.3. Decal Sheet
The SRRX decal sheet contains all logo variants at multiple scales, number sets, road marking elements, and reporting mark blocks for fleet application. The sheet is print-ready and is to be inserted below.

## D.4. Usage Notes
The SRRX motto — *Small Railroad. Big Heart.* — is used sparingly and reserved for public-facing materials only. It does not appear on rolling stock or motive power. The motto is never used in operational documentation.

Color matching for physical modeling applications should reference the hex values above as the primary standard. Paint brand equivalents are to be identified by the modeler and are not specified here, as available products vary by supplier and era.
