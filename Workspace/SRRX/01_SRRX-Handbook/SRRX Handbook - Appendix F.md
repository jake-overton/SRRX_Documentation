# APPENDIX F – SRRX Fleet Roster

This appendix lists all motive power and rolling stock assigned to the Silver Ridge Railway. Decoder addresses, sound profiles, and JMRI roster entries are to be completed following decoder installation and commissioning. Entries marked as pending reflect equipment awaiting DCC conversion or mechanical assessment.

## F.1. Motive Power
* **No. 98**: 70-Ton GE, L&N — DCC operational. Primary yard switcher and mine helper. Capable of independent mine run. Decoder address: 98.
* **44-Ton GE**: Red/gold — DCC installed, bad gears, untested. Status: [assess and confirm serviceability].
* **SP #5017**: EMD GP-30 — DC, decoder candidate. Good runner. Primary road engine and mine run power pending DCC conversion. Underframe inspection required to confirm model. Decoder address: 5017.
* **MoPac #4637**: GE B23-7 — DC, decoder candidate. Designated interchange power. Stationed at interchange track during operating sessions. Decoder address: 4637.
* **Santa Fe F9A #215**: Warbonnet — DC, decoder candidate. Primary Valley Flyer power. White drive gears — extended run test required before regular service. Decoder address: 215 (if DCC capable).
* **Santa Fe F9B #215**: Warbonnet — Dummy unit. When in service, it is coupled to F9A #215 as Valley Flyer A-B set. No decoder required.
* **SP #9725**: Dummy unit. Pairs with SP #5017 as needed.
