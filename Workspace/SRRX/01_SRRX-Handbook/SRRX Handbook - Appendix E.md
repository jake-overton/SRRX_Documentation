# APPENDIX E – Glossary

The following definitions cover operations, equipment, track geography, and SRRX-specific language as used throughout this handbook. Entries reflect the Silver Ridge Railway track plan v10 and canonical naming conventions. General model railroad and DCC terms are included where they appear in operating procedures.

## A
* **Air Test**: A brake test performed before a train departs a terminal or yard.
* **A-B Set**: A paired locomotive consist consisting of a cab-equipped A unit and a cabless B unit.
* **Authority**: Permission granted by the dispatcher for a crew to occupy a defined section of track. On the SRRX, authority is issued via track warrant.

## B
* **B Unit**: A locomotive without an operating cab, designed to work in multiple with an A unit.
* **Ballast**: Crushed rock placed beneath and around track ties to support the rail and aid drainage.
* **Block**: A defined section of track governed by movement authority.
* **Booster District**: An electrically isolated section of layout track fed by its own DCC booster or power district.
* **Branchline**: A secondary route diverging from a main line. The SRRX operates as an independent shortline but functions operationally as a branchline connecting Pine Valley Junction to Silver Ridge.

## C
* **Car Flow**: The planned movement of freight cars between industries across one or more operating sessions. SRRX car flow is governed by the two-visit cycle: cars are spotted, loaded or unloaded, and pulled on a subsequent run.
* **Centerbeam**: A flatcar with a central longitudinal spine, used for dimensional lumber loads. Relevant to Timberline Lumber operations.
* **Consist**: The locomotives assigned to a train, listed in order from rear to front as seen from the engineer's position. The Valley Flyer consist is fixed: Obs #2780 — Coach #1919 — F9A #215.
* **Coupler**: The mechanical device connecting cars and locomotives. The SRRX fleet uses a mix of Rapido-style and knuckle couplers; fleet-wide standardization is a pending project. The observation car carries no rear coupler — this is a permanent constraint.

## D
* **DCC**: Digital Command Control. The electronic system used to operate SRRX locomotives independently on shared track. The SRRX runs DCC-EX with JMRI and Engine Driver.
* **D&RGW**: Denver and Rio Grande Western Railroad. The Class I connection at the SRRX interchange, located in the NW corner of the layout at Pine Valley Junction.
* **Dispatcher**: The person authorizing train movements on the SRRX. In single-operator sessions, the operator serves as both crew and dispatcher.
* **Double-Heading**: Two locomotives pulling from the front of a train.
* **Drop Feeder**: A short wire connecting the rail to the DCC power bus beneath the layout.

## E
* **Engine Driver**: Android application used to operate SRRX locomotives wirelessly via the DCC-EX system. A dedicated Android device is required due to wi-fi conflict issues.
* **Engine Service Track**: Track designated for fueling, sanding, or light servicing of locomotives. Location to be confirmed at construction.
* **EOT Device**: End-of-train marker.

## F
* **F9A / F9B**: EMD F9 locomotive variants. The A unit carries a full cab; the B unit is cabless. SRRX operates an ATSF Warbonnet A-B set as primary Valley Flyer power. The F9A carries white drive gears and requires an extended run test before entering regular service.
* **Flex Track**: Bendable sectional track used for curves and custom alignments in layout construction.
* **Fouling Point**: The location on a spur or siding beyond which a car will interfere with movements on the adjacent track. Fouling point markers are to be installed at track laying per Section 7.9.
* **Freight Window**: A scheduled period of time during which freight operations may occur without conflicting with Valley Flyer passenger movements. See Appendix A.

## G
* **Gondola**: An open-top freight car used for scrap, aggregate, or bulk materials. The SRRX uses an MTL gondola on the River Run for Timberline Lumber service.
* **Gorge Subdivision**: The SRRX operating district covering the Scenic Run route: Pine Valley Junction to Silver Ridge via the National Park District, Timberline Fuel Depot, and Silver Ridge Gorge. Also referred to informally as the Scenic Run.
* **GP38-2 / GP40**: EMD General Purpose locomotive models. MoPac #4637 is a GP38-2 or GP40 serving as SRRX interchange power, stationed at the interchange track during operating sessions.
* **Grade**: The slope of the track expressed as a percentage. The Silver Ridge Mine spur includes grades of 2.7%, 3.3%, and 2.6% across its three switchback legs.

## H
* **Helper**: A locomotive added to a train to assist with steep grades. On the SRRX, No. 98 (70-Ton GE) serves as the primary mine helper.
* **Helper Engine Pocket**: The short siding at Pine Valley Junction where the mine helper locomotive is staged between assignments. Visible on the v10 track plan at the NE end of the PVJ yard.
* **Hopper**: A freight car designed for bulk commodities such as ore or coal, with bottom-discharge doors. Used extensively on the SRRX mine run.

## I
* **Interchange**: The track where the SRRX exchanges cars with the D&RGW. Located in the NW corner of the layout. MoPac #4637 is the designated interchange locomotive. Track length and placement to be confirmed at construction.
* **Industry Spot**: A designated car placement location at a specific industry. Each spot has an implied load state: cars are spotted empty for loading or loaded for pickup, depending on the industry.

## J
* **JMRI**: Java Model Railroad Interface. The SRRX uses JMRI for DCC throttle management and locomotive roster maintenance. Roster completion is pending decoder installation.
* **Joint**: A physical connection between two sections of rail. On the SRRX, joints on curves are soldered for continuity; expansion gaps are cut on straight sections.

## K
* **Kitbash**: The practice of modifying or combining commercial structure kits to produce a unique or site-specific building.

## L
* **Lead Track**: A track used to access a yard or spur, allowing switching moves without fouling the mainline.
* **Loadout**: The facility or track position where ore hoppers are filled at the Silver Ridge Mine. Access is via Spur A.

## M
* **Mainline**: The primary operating track of the SRRX, connecting Pine Valley Junction to Silver Ridge. The mainline forms a continuous loop with two subdivisions: the River Subdivision (clockwise) and the Gorge Subdivision (counterclockwise).
* **Mine Run**: The operating assignment serving the Silver Ridge Mine. Typically handled by No. 98 alone or with SP #5017 as road power on the approach.
* **Milepost (MP)**: A distance marker along the line. Mileposts appear on the layout map (Appendix A) but are not used in handbook prose.
* **Momentum**: A DCC decoder setting that simulates the mass of a locomotive, producing gradual acceleration and deceleration. Recommended for all SRRX road power.

## N
* **National Park District**: The scenic district on the east leg of the layout, traversed by the Gorge Subdivision between the NE tunnels and Timberline Fuel Depot. The Valley Flyer passes through this district on every Scenic Run.
* **Normal Position**: The default alignment of a turnout, typically straight through.
* **N Scale**: 1:160 scale. The modeling scale used for the Silver Ridge Railway.

## O
* **Observation Car**: A passenger car with an open or enclosed rear platform for scenic viewing. The SRRX Valley Flyer operates with Obs #2780 at the PVJ end of the consist.
* **Operations (Ops)**: The practice of running model trains according to prototype-inspired procedures: car forwarding, switching assignments, timetable adherence, and crew responsibilities.

## P
* **Pine Valley District**: The operating district centered on Pine Valley Junction, encompassing the PVJ yard, interchange, warehouse, and station facilities.
* **Pine Valley Junction (PVJ)**: The western terminal of the SRRX, located in the NW corner of the layout. PVJ includes the yard, station spur, helper engine pocket, warehouse, and D&RGW interchange. The Valley Flyer originates here.
* **Power District**: See Booster District.

## R
* **Reporting Marks**: The alphabetic code identifying a railroad or car owner stenciled on freight equipment. SRRX reporting marks appear on company-owned cars.
* **Reversed Position**: A turnout aligned to the diverging route rather than the normal straight-through position.
* **RIP Track**: "Repair In Place" track in PVJ yard used for minor repairs to locomotives and freight cars. 
* **River Subdivision**: The SRRX operating district covering the River Run route: Pine Valley Junction eastbound via the outer NE tunnel, south on the east leg through Timberline Lumber, arriving at Silver Ridge from the east.
* **River Run**: See River Subdivision. Used informally to describe clockwise mainline operations.
* **Road Power**: The locomotive(s) assigned to mainline or through freight service, as distinct from a yard switcher. SP #5017 is the designated SRRX road engine pending decoder installation.
* **Runaround**: A track maneuver allowing a locomotive to move from one end of a car or cut to the other. A mainline runaround is available at Silver Ridge.

## S
* **Scenic Run**: See Gorge Subdivision. Used informally to describe counterclockwise mainline operations via the gorge.
* **SD40**: An EMD six-axle road locomotive. SP #5017 is believed to be an SD40 pending underframe inspection.
* **Silver Ridge Depot**: The passenger and freight station at Silver Ridge, located at the south end of the east leg. The Valley Flyer terminates here.
* **Silver Ridge Gorge**: The dramatic gorge scenery on the Gorge Subdivision, south of Timberline Fuel Depot. A viewing platform is planned on the north rim, pending construction.
* **Silver Ridge Mine**: The ore extraction facility at the NE corner of the layout, accessed via a three-leg switchback spur off the north leg. Spur A holds loaded cars; Spur B holds empties.
* **Silver Ridge National Park District**: See National Park District.
* **Silver Smelter**: An industrial facility on the Mine Subdivision, served by tank cars. Located between the NE tunnel exit and Timberline Fuel Depot.
* **Spur A / Spur B**: The two storage tracks at the Silver Ridge Mine. Spur A is used for loaded or active ore cars; Spur B holds empties. Both spurs were added in track plan v10.
* **Spotting**: The act of placing a freight car at a specific industry location for loading or unloading.
* **Switch / Turnout**: A track device that allows a train to move from one track to another. Used interchangeably in this handbook.
* **Switchback**: A reversing track arrangement used to gain elevation or access a location unreachable by a direct approach. The Silver Ridge Mine spur uses a three-leg switchback.

## T
* **Team Track**: A publicly accessible track at a depot or yard where local businesses can load or unload freight cars without a private industry spur. The Silver Ridge Depot includes a team track; extended track or a second spur is pending construction.
* **Timberline District**: The operating district on the east leg encompassing Timberline Lumber and Timberline Fuel Depot.
* **Timberline Fuel Depot**: The fuel distribution facility on the Gorge Subdivision, North of the gorge. Served by tank cars including the Shell primary and Dow/Hooker secondary cars.
* **Timberline Lumber**: The lumber industry on the River Subdivision, served by the River Run freight assignment. Currently served by a single spur; a second spot is pending construction confirmation.
* **Track Warrant**: A dispatcher-issued written or verbal authority granting a crew the right to occupy a specific section of track during a defined time window.
* **Two-Visit Cycle**: The SRRX car forwarding convention in which a freight car requires two separate operating sessions to complete a spot: it is placed empty on the first visit and retrieved loaded (or vice versa) on the second.
* **Tunnel**: The SRRX has two tunnels at the NE corner of the layout. The outer tunnel carries the River Subdivision; the inner tunnel carries the Gorge Subdivision.

## U
* **Uncoupling**: The separation of two cars or a car and locomotive. Performed manually or with a magnetic uncoupling ramp depending on coupler type.
* **Underframe**: The structural base of a freight car, including bolsters, center sill, and truck mounting points. SP #5017 requires an underframe check to confirm locomotive model and decoder compatibility.

## V
* **Valley Flyer**: The SRRX named passenger train operating between Pine Valley Junction and Silver Ridge via the Gorge Subdivision. Consist is fixed: Obs #2780 — Coach #1919 — F9A #215. The Valley Flyer has priority over all freight movements.

## W
* **Waybill**: A document describing a freight car's origin, destination, commodity, and routing. SRRX waybills are the primary car forwarding tool. See Appendix C.
* **Weathering**: Painting and finishing techniques applied to models to simulate age, wear, and environmental exposure.
* **Wye**: A triangular track arrangement used to turn locomotives or entire trains. The SRRX does not have a wye; turning is not required in normal operations.

## Y
* **Yard**: A facility with multiple tracks for sorting, storing, and assembling cars. Pine Valley Junction contains the primary SRRX yard.
* **Yard Limits**: The defined boundary of a yard within which yard crews may move without track warrant authority.
* **Yard Office**: The administrative structure at the PVJ yard.
