# SRRX TRAFFIC SYSTEMS & OPERATIONAL PHILOSOPHY
# Authority: Operations Management

## 1. OPERATIONAL PHILOSOPHY
* **System Type:** Free-flowing, continuous running and switching layout. 
* **Purpose:** Operating sessions are designed as an open framework with a historical backstory to drive train routing, rather than rigid, timetable-constrained fast-clock rules.

## 2. THE VALLEY FLYER COMMANDS
* **Consist Identity:** The primary passenger flagship of the line (officially represented by EMD GP7/GP9 Locomotive SRRX 102 in deep maroon with silver-white striping).
* **Seasonal Routing Directives:**
    * **Summer Tourist Mode:** The Valley Flyer departs the Silver Ridge Depot, runs through the outer tunnel trackage, and navigates the high scenic loop before terminating/pausing at the Pine Valley Junction (PVJ) depot or the yard tracks.
    * **Winter Transit Mode:** Due to simulated mountain snow blockage, the train bypasses the outer loop entirely. It limits its run to the protected inner gorge run and the inner tunnel, acting as a winter commuter/mail link between the two towns.

## 3. INDUSTRY REGISTER
* **Pine Valley Junction Yard:** Central clearing throat for classifying rolling stock.
* **The Mine Spur:** High-grade industrial spur serving Rocky Mountain ore and mineral loading facilities.
