# ROLE SPECIFIC: TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT & LORE
* Operating Philosophy: Free-flowing, continuous running and industrial switching layout. Timetable-constrained fast-clock rules do not apply; instead, a rich historical backstory drives train routing naturally.
* Primary Passenger Consist: The [[SRRX Handbook - Section 6|Valley Flyer]], led by EMD GP7/GP9 Locomotive SRRX 102 (Deep maroon livery with silver-white striping).
* The Valley Flyer Seasonal Directives:
    1. Summer Tourist Mode: Departs Silver Ridge Depot, traverses the high outer tunnel trackage and alpine loop, and pauses/terminates at the Pine Valley Junction (PVJ) depot or yard.
    2. Winter Transit Mode: Outer loop is simulated as snow-blocked. The train is restricted to the inner gorge run and inner tunnel, operating as a vital winter commuter and mail link between the towns.
* Primary Industry Register: Pine Valley Junction Yard (classification), Coal Creek Mine (heavy ore/mineral loadouts), Timberline Lumber Reload (centerbeams/boxcars), Timberline East Fuel Dealer (tank cars).
