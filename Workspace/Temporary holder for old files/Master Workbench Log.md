# SRRX Master Workbench Log
**Setting:** Rocky Mountains (Late 1970s – Early 2000s) | **Scale:** N-Scale (1:160)

## Structure Paint & Finishing Directory

### The Bank Building (June 2026 Build)
* **Main Upper Walls & Corner Quoins:** Micro-Mark Chalky Limestone (Acrylic). Applied via airbrush with a few drops of flow improver.
* **First Floor Masonry & Mullions:** Micro-Mark Undercoat Lt. Gray (Acrylic). Switched from the originally planned Reefer Gray because Reefer Gray felt too dark/heavy for the N-scale foundation.
* **First Floor Window Recesses:** Left in Chalky Limestone to provide bright, clean architectural contrast behind the glass.
* **Second Floor Window Casements & Parapet Accents:** Hand-brushed with Undercoat Lt. Gray to pull the foundation color upward and pop the 3D-printed details.
* **Balcony Entryway:** Top side left in Chalky Limestone (second-floor stone extension); underside painted gray to match the ground-floor entryway ceiling line.
* **Strategic Lesson:** For massive structural base coats, avoid wrestling with tiny hobby bottles and flow-improver ratios. Take a color swatch to a hardware store (Ace/Home Depot) and buy a sample quart of flat latex paint. Thin it with DIY thinner (water/glycerin mix) for a fast, clog-free blast.

### Pergande's Brewery
* **Technique:** Used an India ink and isopropyl alcohol mix as a base stain to age and weather the bare wood components (specifically the corner beams and interior braces).

### Flagstop Depot & Privy
* **Depot Main Body Siding:** Bare wood sheets to receive the India ink/alcohol wash first, followed by a light layer of railroad brown. Skip primer entirely on the wood siding so the paint can be dry-brushed or scraped to reveal the weathered gray grain underneath.
* **Privy Windows:** To be painted using the leftover jar of SRRX Burgundy to create a subtle visual anchor to the nearby Gorge-ous Grub diner.
* **Roof Vents:** Leave as manufactured (pre-painted silver/galvanized steel look).

### Overton Passenger Coach
* **Body Color:** Micro-Mark Mossy Green (Olive Green prototype match). 
* **Preparation:** Must be primed first using the Micro-Mark gray airbrush primer (do not use heavy hardware-store rattle cans, as they risk flooding fine N-scale rivet/panel lines).
* **Roof:** Leave factory black to simulate weathered heavyweight asphalt/canvas.

## Kitbashing & Mechanical Fixes

### 3D-Printed Kit Tolerances (The Bank)
* **The Issue:** Internal floor/ceiling plates did not square up or glue well due to standard 3D-printing expansion/warping.
* **The Fix:** Manually shaved approximately 1/16" off the floor/ceiling plate dimensions to force a clean exterior wall fit.
* **Lighting Entry:** A central hole was drilled into the floor/ceiling plate to accommodate a layout LED.
* **Assembly State:** The floor/ceiling plate remains unglued; it is held in place entirely by friction from the interior styrene backing and window glazing, allowing for easy future electrical servicing.
* **Roof Access:** The roof deck is a tight friction fit within the parapet walls. Small magnets were glued to the inside of the structure. An external magnet can be used as a "handle" to pull the roof out without scratching the exterior paint.
* **Window Glazing Save:** When kit window materials are missing or short, clear acetate can be cleanly cannibalized from the outer retail box packaging of other kits (e.g., Blair Line/N-Scale Architect boxes) without ruining the kit contents inside.

## Airbrush Maintenance Log
* **The "Air Bubbling/No Flow" Fix:** When the brush is spraying clean water/alcohol but clogs instantly on thinned acrylics, check the drop-in nozzle O-ring. If it has swelled from an alcohol soak, it breaks the critical air seal.
* **Action:** Completely disassemble the nozzle assembly and let the O-ring dry out completely to reset its shape before reassembling.

## 🪵 Laser-Cut Wood Processing Techniques

### Warping Mitigation
* **Two-Sided Wash Rule:** Always apply the India ink/alcohol wash to both the front and back sides of the flat laser sheets simultaneously. Equalizing the moisture prevents the thin wood fibers from bowing or warping as the alcohol evaporates, ensuring the pieces remain perfectly flat for assembly.

* **Rolling Stock:** 1x Re-purposed plastic Overton Coach body, roof, and interior glazing.

### Critical Bench Warnings
* **Material Swelling:** Because laser-cut components, tabs, and slots are engineered to tight tolerances, wood fibers can swell significantly from water/alcohol-based washes. Mandatory dry test-fitting and light sanding/shaving are required before introducing adhesive to internal slots.
* **Adhesive Strategy:** Apply wood glue or yellow carpenter's glue strictly from the interior matrix of the structure to prevent squeeze-out from altering the exterior weathered ink appearance. Secure joints with temporary masking tape during curing.
