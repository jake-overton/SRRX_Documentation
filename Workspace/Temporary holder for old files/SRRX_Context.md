## 3. Structural Barriers & Freeze Constraints

To prevent mechanical or structural interference beneath the benchwork, the following parameters are strictly enforced:

* **Sub-Plywood Obstructions:** Laminated 2x4 leg blocks (measuring 3.0" x 3.5") and 1x3 vertical keeper joists (2.75" deep) run completely flush against the underside of the plywood subroadbed.
* **The 3-Inch Exclusion Rule:** If any proposed wire path, switch machine placement, or structural cutout comes within a **3-inch radius** of a framing barrier (leg blocks or keeper joists), or introduces a vertical stack conflict, the planning line must **FREEZE** until physical clearance is verified or overridden by the user.

---

## 4. Electrical & DCC Standards (`Sparky`)

* **Primary DCC Bus:** 14 AWG stranded copper wire, red/black parallel zip wire to eliminate DCC signal noise.
* **Sub-Bus Distribution:** Terminal blocks for branch power from the primary bus to localized zone sub-buses.
* **Short Circuit Protection:** Every zone sub-bus must route directly through an inline **1003 automotive tail-light bulb terminal block assembly** for visual isolation before final track distribution.
* **Track Feeders:** 22 AWG solid copper wire dropped from local terminal blocks directly to the rail base. Maximum allowable length where possible is **6 to 12 inches**.
* **Turnout Automation Power:** 13 Circuitron Tortoise slow-motion switch machines driven by a dedicated, completely isolated DC accessory power bus (zero connection to the DCC track bus).

---

## 5. Trackwork & Turnout Matrix (`Lieutenant Layout`)

* **Track Standards:** Atlas sectional and flex track utilized for all mainline trackage, passing sidings, and industrial spurs.
* **Turnouts:** Mainline turnouts are Atlas N-scale. The classification yard ladder throat utilizes Peco N-scale turnouts exclusively.
* **Roadbed Profiles:** 1/8" Atlas cork roadbed on the mainline. Sub-yard tracks and the ladder throat are (possibly) laid flat directly onto the pink foam surface.
* **Western Throat Alignment:** The yard ladder tracks run on a Northwest-to-Southeast diagonal, requiring a specific Southwest-to-Northeast mechanical stroke for the turnout throwbars.

## 6. Landscape, 3D Fabrication, & Finishes (`Professor Paint & Sally Scenery`)

* **The Mine Spur Vertical Window:** Located along the North Leg loop. The track climbs sharply on custom risers directly over the surface-linkage assembly of Turnout #3. At its closest crossing point, the bottom of the elevated Mine Spur roadbed structure must maintain exactly **0.7" of vertical clearance** above the top surface of the 1" pink foam.
* **3D Fabrication Standards:** Lineside structures, figures, and Sears-style architectural kits are custom-printed in PLA filament. All prints must be thoroughly sanded, coated with fill-primer to eliminate visible layer lines, and sealed before applying final paint.
* **Visual Concealment Protocol:** All visible trackage must receive a weathered grimy black/rust rail profile. Top-surface mechanical linkages, torsion sleeves, and actuator wires must be painted flat grimy black or physically masked using scenery elements (ballast, tool shacks, dense foliage).

## 7. Operational Parameters & Rolling Stock Lore

* **Operating Philosophy:** Continuous, free-flowing mainline running paired with localized industrial switching. Fast-clock timetable rules do not apply; operational movements are guided by historical narrative.
* **Primary Passenger Consist:** *The Valley Flyer*, pulled by EMD GP7/GP9 Locomotive **SRRX 102** dressed in a deep maroon livery with silver-white striping.
* **Seasonal Operational Directives:**
1. *Summer Tourist Mode:* *The Valley Flyer* departs Silver Ridge Depot, traverses the high outer tunnel trackage and alpine loop, and pauses or terminates at the Pine Valley Junction (PVJ) depot/yard.
2. *Winter Transit Mode:* The high outer alpine loop is simulated as completely snow-blocked. The consist is restricted to the inner gorge run and inner tunnel, operating as a critical winter commuter and mail lifeline between settlements.


* **Primary Industry Register:**
* *Pine Valley Junction Yard:* Freight classification and block building.
* *Coal Creek Mine:* Heavy ore and mineral loadouts.
* *Timberline Lumber Reload:* Inbound/outbound centerbeams and boxcars.
* *Timberline East Fuel Dealer:* Dedicated tank car spots.
